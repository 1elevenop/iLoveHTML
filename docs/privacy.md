# iLoveHTML Privacy Policy

This is the same text the app shows in Settings → About → Privacy Policy
(source: `app/lib/l10n/app_en.arb`, key `privacyBody`). Last checked against
release 1.2.3 on 2026-11-11.

iLoveHTML is built to work on your device. This policy explains what that means for your data.

## Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The author receives no data about you or your projects.

## Data stored on your device

Your projects, website files, icons, build outputs and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Release keys stay on your device too: on Android they are encrypted with the Android Keystore, and on Windows their passwords are encrypted for your Windows account. Uninstalling the app deletes this data, so keep backups.

## Network use

The app connects to the internet only when you ask it to: to check or preview a website URL you entered, to open links such as Buy Me a Coffee in your browser, and, on Windows, when Gradle downloads build tools. The Install on phone feature serves the file on your local network for 10 minutes, only while its window is open.

## Update check

If "Check for updates" is on (it is by default), about twice a day iLoveHTML asks GitHub (api.github.com) for the latest iLoveHTML release, to tell you when a new version is out. The request contains no account, no identifiers and nothing about your projects; like any website, GitHub sees your IP address. Turn it off in Settings → About.

## Apps you build

Apps made with iLoveHTML contain no tracking from iLoveHTML. What they collect depends on the website you put in them, and you are responsible for their privacy policy.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data, from children or anyone else.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated with new versions of the app; the date above shows the latest change. Questions: open an issue on the iLoveHTML GitHub project by 1elevenop.
